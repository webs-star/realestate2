🔍 How RealPlot Connect Works

Browse all listings freely. To view the contact details and use the real-time map that guides you to the exact physical location of a listing, simply pay a small fee. Once paid, you gain full access for 24 hours.

  • 📞 Direct contact with the property owner or agent
  • 🗺️ Real-time map navigation to the exact property location
  • ⏱️ 24 hours of uninterrupted access after payment
  • 📱 Easy call and chat tools for faster communication

💡 The access fee helps us reduce fake inquiries, protect owner privacy, and maintain accurate and verified listings.

💳 Access Fees

The fee depends on the property price range — lower-priced listings have a lower fee, and higher-value properties have a slightly higher fee. This is to balance fairness and value for both renters and buyers.

After payment, you’ll be directed to the page with full access to:

  • ✔ Owner contact details
  • ✔ Real-time map with directions
  • ✔ Chat and call functionality
  • ✔ Ability to browse all matching listings freely

No subscriptions — one small fee for a full 24-hour access window.
